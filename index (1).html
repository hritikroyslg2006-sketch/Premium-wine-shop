A Legacy of Uncompromising Taste

The Velvet Vine was founded on a simple conviction: exceptional wine deserves an exceptional home. For over three decades, we have sought out the world's most extraordinary vineyards, building lasting relationships with winemakers who share our reverence for the craft.

Each bottle in our cellar is chosen not merely for its score or provenance, but for the story it carries — the specific microclimate, the hands that tended the vines, the patience that shaped every vintage into something transcendent.

We invite you to explore our curated collection and find the wine that speaks to you.
